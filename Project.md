# 🌱 GrowthLog — Personal Growth Tracking & Manifestation Journal

> *Record who you are today. Manifest who you want to become. Watch yourself grow.*

---

## 📌 What is GrowthLog?

GrowthLog is a personal development web application designed to help you track, measure, and visualize your growth across every area of your life — career, relationships, emotional health, personality, and more.

The core belief behind GrowthLog is simple:

> **You cannot improve what you do not measure. You cannot become who you cannot see.**

Most people want to grow but have no record of their journey. GrowthLog fixes that. It gives you a daily space to log your progress, set meaningful goals, reflect on your experiences, and look back to see — in concrete terms — how far you have come.

---

## 🎯 Who is this for?

- Anyone who wants to grow intentionally and not just let life happen to them
- People who journal but want structure and visibility into their patterns
- Goal-setters who want to track progress beyond a to-do list
- Anyone going through a transformation — career switch, healing, self-improvement, relationship growth
- People who want to manifest a future version of themselves and hold themselves accountable to it

## 🗺️ Full Project Flow

### STEP 1 — Onboarding
```
User signs up
       ↓
Chooses life categories to track
(Career / Emotional / Relationships / Personality / Custom)
       ↓
Writes a "Who I am today" Snapshot
       ↓
Writes their first Manifestation
"Who do I want to become in the next 30/60/90 days?"
       ↓
Sets their daily reminder time
       ↓
Lands on their personal Dashboard
```

---

### STEP 2 — Daily Check-in (The Heartbeat)
```
User opens app each day
       ↓
Sees today's check-in card for each category
       ↓
For each category:
  - Writes what they did, learned, or felt
  - Rates their mood and energy (1–10)
  - Tags specific emotions (optional)
  - Answers a daily prompt (optional)
       ↓
Submits the day's log
       ↓
Streak counter updates
       ↓
App acknowledges the log with a milestone
if applicable (7 days, 30 days, etc.)
```

---

### STEP 3 — Goal Setting & Tracking
```
User creates a Goal inside any Category
       ↓
Sets a title, description, and deadline
       ↓
Optionally breaks it into micro-goals
(weekly milestones toward the big goal)
       ↓
Each day, the goal is visible in that category's check-in
       ↓
On deadline day, user is prompted:
  ┌─────────────────────────────────┐
  │  Did you achieve this goal?     │
  │                                 │
  │  ✅ Yes → Write what you learned│
  │  🔄 Extend → Give a reason +    │
  │              set new deadline   │
  │  ❌ Abandon → Reflect on why    │
  └─────────────────────────────────┘
       ↓
Goal moves to history with full reflection attached
```

---

### STEP 4 — Growth Dashboard
```
User visits Dashboard anytime
       ↓
Selects a time range:
  Last 7 days / 30 days / 90 days / Custom
       ↓
Sees:
  - Consistency heatmap (which days were logged)
  - Category-wise growth summary
  - Mood & energy trends over time
  - Goal completion rate
  - Emotion frequency patterns
  - Most consistent category vs. most neglected
       ↓
Drills into any category for detailed view
       ↓
Reads past log entries on a timeline
```

---

### STEP 5 — Manifestation Cycle
```
User writes a Manifestation:
"In 60 days I want to be someone who..."
       ↓
App records the start date and target date
       ↓
Every week, app shows a gentle reminder
of the vision the user set
       ↓
On Day N (target date), app prompts:
  - Here is what you wrote 60 days ago
  - Here is what you actually logged
  - How close did you get?
  - Write your reflection
       ↓
User starts a new Manifestation cycle
with updated vision based on growth
```

---

### STEP 6 — Insights & Patterns (AI Layer)
```
App analyses logs over time
       ↓
Generates weekly insight:
  "You were most consistent in Career this week.
   Your mood tends to dip on Wednesdays.
   You've extended the same goal 3 times — 
   let's look at why."
       ↓
Monthly deeper report:
  - What patterns emerged
  - Which emotions dominated
  - Category balance check
  - Growth highlights
       ↓
AI generates a "Letter from your Future Self"
based on your manifestation + actual logs
```

## 🔁 The Daily Loop (In Plain English)

```
Morning  →  Get reminded to check in
Daytime  →  Live your day with your goals in mind
Evening  →  Open GrowthLog (2–3 minutes)
            Log what happened in each category
            Rate your mood and energy
            Mark any goal progress
```
