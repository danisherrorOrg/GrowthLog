## Suggested Build Order
 
### Phase 1 — Foundations (Week 1)
- Daily journal, mood tracker, energy levels, day rating
- Sleep tracker, morning & night routine log
- Reminders, dark mode, offline mode, encryption
 
### Phase 2 — Structure (Week 2)
- Habit tracker + streaks + streak protection
- Goal setting & goal progress
- Life wheel / radar chart
 
### Phase 3 — Accountability (Week 3)
- Time tracking, task completion
- Daily spending & income tracking
- People log, quality time log
 
### Phase 4 — Depth (Week 4–6)
- Health details: exercise, water, weight, steps
- Career wins, skills tracker, books log
- Emotional check-in, mindfulness, anxiety tracker
 
### Phase 5 — Intelligence (Month 2+)
- Trend charts, life score, correlation finder
- Best/worst days analysis, happiness drivers
- Year in review, AI life coach, sentiment analysis
