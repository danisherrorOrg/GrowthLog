**CORE FEATURES (Build Now)**

**Categories & Goals**
- Create custom life categories (Personality, Career, Emotional, Relationships, etc.)
- Set goals inside each category with a target date
- Mark goals as complete, extend deadline with a reason, or reflect on what was learned
- Archive old goals without deleting them

**Daily Check-in**
- A simple daily log for each category — what did you do, learn, or feel today?
- Mood/energy rating per day (1–10 scale)
- Streak counter — how many days in a row you've checked in
- Reminder nudge if you haven't logged by a certain time

**Growth Dashboard**
- See your progress over last 7, 30, 60, 90 days or custom range
- Category-wise growth summary
- Goal completion rate over time
- Visual timeline of your journey

**Manifestation Board**
- Write who you want to become in N days
- On day N, the app shows your vision vs. your actual logged growth
- Re-manifest for the next N days based on new learnings

---

**ENGAGEMENT FEATURES (Build Soon)**

**Insights & Reflections**
- Weekly auto-generated summary — "This week you were most consistent in Career and least in Emotional"
- Monthly reflection prompts — guided questions to go deeper
- Pattern detection — "You tend to skip logs on Mondays" or "Your mood dips mid-week"

**Progress Visualization**
- Heat map (like GitHub's contribution graph) showing your daily activity
- Spider/radar chart showing balance across all life categories
- Growth curve per category over time
- Milestone markers on your timeline

**Smart Prompts**
- Daily prompt suggestions if you don't know what to write — "What made you uncomfortable today and what did you learn from it?"
- Category-specific prompts — emotional prompts vs. career prompts feel different
- Rotating quotes or principles aligned to your categories

**Streaks & Milestones**
- Celebrate streaks — 7 days, 30 days, 100 days
- Milestone badges for completing your first goal, logging for a month, etc.
- "Comeback" recognition — acknowledges when you return after a gap without shaming you

---

**DEPTH FEATURES (Next Phase)**

**Goal Decomposition**
- Break a big goal into weekly micro-goals
- Each micro-goal feeds into the parent goal's progress
- See which micro-habits are driving the most growth

**Before & After Snapshots**
- Write a "who I am today" snapshot when you start
- After 30/60/90 days, write again and compare side by side
- Photo or voice note attachments for richer snapshots

**Deadline Intelligence**
- When you extend a deadline, the app tracks how many times and why
- Over time it shows you patterns — "You tend to overestimate what you can do in 2 weeks"
- Smarter goal-setting nudges based on your history

**Emotion Tracking**
- Log specific emotions per day, not just mood score
- See emotional trends — "You felt anxious 6 times this month, mostly on workdays"
- Link emotions to categories — what areas of life affect your mood most

**FUTURE / ADVANCED FEATURES**

**AI-Powered Layer**
- AI reads your logs and gives you a weekly growth report in plain language
- Spots things you can't see yourself — recurring themes, blind spots, growth areas
- Suggests which category needs more attention based on your patterns
- Generates a "letter from your future self" based on your manifested vision

**Accountability Partner**
- Invite one trusted person to see your progress (not your raw logs, just summary)
- They can send you encouragement or a gentle nudge
- Optional — completely private by default

**Templates & Frameworks**
- Pre-built category templates — "Career Switch in 90 Days", "Emotional Healing Journey", "Fitness Transformation"
- Import a framework like journaling methods or productivity systems as a starting structure

**Export & Memory**
- Export your full journey as a PDF — a book of your growth
- "On this day last year" memory feature
- Year in review — auto-generated annual summary

**Voice Logging**
- Speak your daily log instead of typing
- Transcribed automatically and saved as text
- Faster check-ins on busy days

**Offline Mode**
- Log even without internet, syncs when back online
- Never miss a day because of connectivity
