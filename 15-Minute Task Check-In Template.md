## Quick 5-Question Version
 
*Use this when you only have 2 minutes for a check-in.*
 
1. Is this the most important thing I can be doing right now?
2. Am I making real progress, or am I going in circles?
3. What will I have to show for this in the next 30 minutes?
4. Is my energy level suited to this task?
5. Should I continue, pause, or drop this task?
---
 
## Weekly Review (Every Friday)
 
Use these to reflect on patterns across the week.
 
- Which tasks did I keep continuing that I should have dropped earlier?
- Which tasks did I drop that turned out to be more important than I thought?
- What kind of tasks drain my energy the most?
- What kind of tasks give me the most flow and output?
- What will I do differently next week based on this?
---
 
*Template designed for 15-minute interval self-review. Repeat every session.*
